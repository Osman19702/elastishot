# Code of conduct

Elastishot is a small project and this is a short document.

Be direct about the work and kind to the people. Review code, not authors.
Assume good faith, ask before assuming, and disagree without being
disagreeable. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and
publishing someone's private information are not tolerated anywhere the
project is discussed: issues, pull requests, discussions and e-mail.

If something crosses that line, e-mail osman.turalioglu@outlook.com. Reports
are handled privately; the maintainer may edit or remove content, and may
block people who keep at it.
